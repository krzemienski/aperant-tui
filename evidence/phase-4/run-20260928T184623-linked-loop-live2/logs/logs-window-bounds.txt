LogsView window bound — derived from stdout.rows, not a fixed constant

A fixed 30-row window disagreed with App's `stdout.rows - 1` frame clip.
On a short terminal the renderer drew fewer rows than the scroll bound
assumed, so the newest events could never be scrolled into view.

terminal 24 rows -> window 15 | maxOffset 4201 | last of 4216 lines reachable: True
terminal 12 rows -> window  3 | maxOffset 4213 | last of 4216 lines reachable: True
